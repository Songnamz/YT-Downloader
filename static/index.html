Shortcut from YouTube

    
While watching a video, click the address bar, put the cursor at the very start and type
      this-site/ in front of the link, then press Enter:

    
this-site/https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6QZVNjpkyLY

    
Or drag this button to your bookmarks bar and click it on any YouTube page:
